# 🏥Healthcare Performance Insights: Growth, Trends & Opportunities 

# 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive healthcare performance dashboard built using Tableau, analyzing the operational and financial performance of multiple healthcare clinics across a region.

The dashboard focuses on revenue, profitability, patient trends, satisfaction, and specialty-level performance, helping stakeholders identify growth opportunities and optimize healthcare delivery.

# 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
💰 Revenue & Profitability

Strong overall profitability with a ~70% profit margin

Dermatology ($1.76M) and Cardiology ($1.67M) are the most profitable specialties

📉 Patient Trends

Patient volume dipped in March (1,292 patients)

Strong rebound leading to a December peak (3,766 patients)

Indicates potential seasonality in healthcare demand

😊 Patient Satisfaction

Satisfaction remained consistently high between 85.57% – 94.5%

Suggests stable service quality across clinics and specialties

❤️ Specialty-Level Performance

Cardiology & Dermatology lead in:

Follow-up visits

Revenue contribution

Profitability

Orthopedics & Pediatrics show:

Lower patient retention

Opportunity for targeted engagement and growth strategies
